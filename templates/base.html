Put SCI Connect on your home screen

    Don't see Add to Home Screen?
    • Open it in Safari. If you came from a text, Facebook, Gmail or Google, open the Safari app (blue compass), type sciconnect.net and try again.
    • Turn off Private Browsing. If Safari's bar is dark, tap the tabs button and switch to a normal tab.
    • Scroll down in the Share menu, or tap More. Still missing? Tap Edit Actions at the bottom and turn on Add to Home Screen.

    Your screen may look a little different depending on your phone or computer, its settings, and updates. Look for the button with the same name.

    Then look for the fox on your home screen. Tap it any time to open SCI Connect.
